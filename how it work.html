Three steps. Three winners.
Zero waste.

Here's the full journey of a meal &mdash from a restaurant kitchen to your dinner table or a community plate.

1

Restaurant uploads food

At the end of service, the kitchen photographs surplus dishes, sets a discounted price, and chooses a pickup window.

2

User orders discounted food

Nearby customers view new listings, order in seconds, and pick up their meals or schedule a fast delivery.

3

Charity receives donations

Any unclaimed food is promptly offered to charity partners, helping reduce waste while supporting those in need.

The result, in one sentence

"Restaurants stop wasting food, customers eat better for less, and charities get the supply they need every single day."
